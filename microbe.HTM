📖 How to Use — Microbial Inventory System
This system helps you track microbial materials — what came in, what went out, how much is left, and when to reorder. You do not need any technical knowledge to use it.
Step 1: Adding New Stock (Inward)
Click the Inward tab at the top. Fill in: which microbe, container code, quantity (kg), batch code, expiry date, and the rack location. Click Submit Inward. The stock is now in the system and visible on the Storage Map.
Step 2: Checking Current Stock (Stock Summary)
Click Stock Summary to see all microbes. Each row shows: kg remaining, number of containers, when the nearest batch expires. Click View Containers to drill into a specific microbe and see every container with full batch history.
Step 3: Issuing Stock (Issuance)
Click the Issuance tab. Enter the product name, customer, DI number, and batch. Click + Add Microbe for each microbe. Type the name, required quantity, and required CFU/g. The system automatically calculates which containers to pick from (FEFO — earliest expiry first). Click Confirm & Issue All to deduct stock.
Step 4: Printing a Picklist & Labels
After the system calculates the containers, click 📋 Picklist to get a printable document showing exactly which rack/shelf/position to go to and how much to pick. Click 🏷 Labels to print thermal labels (100×50mm) for the picked material. Hand the picklist to the store person and give to QC to verify.
Step 5: Viewing the Storage Map
Click the Storage tab to see all 15 racks visually. Green = Fresh Blue = Moderate Amber = Near Expiry Red = Expired Grey = Exhausted. Click any coloured cell to see full container details.
Step 6: Understanding the Dashboard
The Dashboard is the home page. Reorder Signals tells you which microbes need purchasing and how urgently (shows how many days of stock remain). Consumption Velocity shows which microbes are used most heavily. Market Intelligence tracks customer buying patterns.
Step 7: Searching History
Click the History tab. Type any microbe name, container code, DI number, or customer name in the search box. Filter by Inward Only or Outward Only. Set a date range if needed. Click any row to expand and see full transaction details. Nothing is ever deleted.
Step 8: Making a Correction (Supervisor Only)
If a quantity was entered wrong, click the faint 🔧 icon in the top-right corner. Enter your 4-digit PIN. This unlocks Correction Mode. A 🔧 icon will appear next to each batch and issuance entry — click it to edit, select a reason, and save. Every correction is logged. Nothing is deleted — only amended with a full record of what changed.
Location Codes Explained
Every storage location has a code like R03-S5-B-L. It means: R03 = Rack 3 → S5 = Shelf 5 (count from bottom) → B = Back row (against the wall) → L = Left position. Other options: F = Front row, M = Middle, R = Right. Example: R07-S2-F-R = Rack 7, Shelf 2, Front row, Right position.
